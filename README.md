# MST 0055 Teaching Experiment (Fall 2026)

A teaching experiment in *MST 0055 Data Management and Python Programming* at BI Norwegian Business School, testing whether mandatory working requirements and a supervised examination restore the link between effort, learning, and assessment after generative AI made the previous take-home format unreliable.

Between 2021 and 2025 — when the course ran under its earlier code, GRA 4142 — the A-share on the take-home examinations rose from 39% to 97%, while attendance, questions asked in class, and voluntary practice drifted slowly downwards over the same years. The course is built on active learning — students spend much of each session working on exercises — and that has continued to work well; the concern is the gradual trend, not the state of any single cohort. From Fall 2026 the course requires 75% attendance (swappable for documented programming practice) and a minimum score on Codewars, and the 30-hour take-home exam is replaced by a 5-hour supervised, hands-on exam.
